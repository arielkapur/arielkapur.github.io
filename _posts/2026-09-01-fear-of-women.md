Special thanks to Isaiah De Hoog [ig @isaiahjdehoog] for the encouragement to write and share thoughts, in the name of learning, and knowledge, our ever beautiful mistress.

We often think about the universe in terms of love.

That’s how life happens - fusion, fundamentally - whether it’s biological or nuclear.

Which is to say - we are all stardust: the result of the aftermath of collisions in the cosmic womb.

Temptation exists for me yet, as such. I am not thrilled to have memories of what it is like to be with a woman, or to know a woman so well as I have - and yet there will always be more to discover beyond my imagination.

I am simply a man with an analogy. I am one half, whose line lacks the means for propagation of a certain chromosome, of certain qualities that have been held so dear and cared for, to time.

I have lost many opportunities to get to know women. I am afraid. Yet, today, when I escaped for a cigarette, I found myself justified once after all in conversing with a woman, as I often do when in the company of cigarette-smoke.

I ended up telling a story of a ghost. The story reminded me of my father, and his time in the military, which he so little has spoken of. I am encouraged by this thought, the spiritual fervor overcomes me, that same sensation of ‘flow-state’, where my hairs stand on end, when I recite to myself: मेरा नाम है भूत. “My name is Ghost”.

I saw tears in the woman’s eyes, as she had within her mind ghost stories of her own. Is this the evocation? Is this the result I have on a woman when I am honest? Truly a crass generalisation, yet a passing thought to be cherished nonetheless.

It brings me comfort to know she lives with another.

To me it seems all women live with another.

The passing loves in my life, only two, have now long gone, yet spiritual scars remain. My soul has unravelled in psychiatric ward admissions, five over the course of the last five years, and regrettable nights in jail cells, including one particularly fond memory in the Surry Hills jail. “Whistling man” in the distance brought me peace, I could tell he was from the Islands from the sounds he made, the evocation was that of an Island woman. Birdcalls.

I never had set eyes on him, but there is a spiritual truth in that - music itself paints a picture using time, transforming our perception of time as we Schrödinger-spiral through the delicate web of reality.

Gumption, my mother said, that night in the Coles when she purchased for me many items, including the lamb that sits in the fridge now, ready to be cleaved by my Global G12 cleaver, which was purchased in the hopes of cooking for my beloved.

Gumption is a cleaning product, yellow, green and yellow packaging. Its texture is rough, like sand. There is a satisfying quality about it, Hayden Christiansen references aside.

The Clone Wars were supposedly great, yet I never set eyes on them too keenly.

There was a girl I once knew who would have jumped at the chance to watch Clone Wars with me. Then, I ended up watching it, rather absentmindedly and disengaged from the albeit beautifully 3d-animated visual content. I watched it in this place near Liverpool, when I was truly under the illusion that my old marijuana salesman, or “plug”, had interest in starting a music studio with me in Liverpool.

This was a good way to waste $2400, I think to myself. I wish him the best. He pursues audio engineering. I am an artist myself, yet I can never seem to get the timing quite right. All I do is write melodies, nearly all day, if I could.

Then, there are the secrets I could never tell, for fear of the right, or wrong person, reading this. No such thing as the wrong person. I do aim for transparency, yet I still hide behind these words, and if we met, I would hide behind my body, and if you knew my body, I would hide in the spirit. And then we would know each other.

“But we already do”, I cry, trapped in this lonely cage.

I disrespected women who tried. Sort of as a social experiment, but not consciously. Just to evoke a reaction - the hypothesis is always departure.

It bears no surprise that I prove myself right time and time again. Departure is an inevitability, yet arrival is a circumstance that I am unprepared to bear, so, dear reader, forgive me.

Forgive me for my lack of a standard.

I am not a soothsayer. The word bears in itself a quality of anti-doomer edge, in that it contains as a prefix ‘sooth-’, which sounds like soothe. And to be soothed and calmed is the dream of every child. So, thus, I wish I were a soothsayer.

And wishes come true like in that Peter Pan movie - the power of shared belief brought Tinkerbell to life.

I do often share my beliefs, yet fairies who cross my path in abundance now no longer reach out with the desperation they once did, for fear that they cannot reach that place. That place of cold which I have come to know so well - as my new lover. My only delicate caress that tells me my place in this universe, which is to say, passing through, a visitor, temporary. временная, as described by that one Soundcloud song on my alt account, which had an Eastern European ghastly abandoned vibe about it, like modern brutalist architecture towering in snow.
We often think in terms of hate.

We stick to things we know, and we are afraid of the dark, as all children have been, at some stage. Our fear defines us, for without fear, we would not survive. Yet, fear and love are two sides of the same coin. For what enables survival is one and the same thing - the chaos the universe brings to bear, knowledge being the only antidote, sensation the only drug that can reveal the truth, a priori.

To mull then, for it is, in my view, uncouth to bring up Kant’s immortalisation of the Latin without mentioning - a posteriori. To create - an endless feedback loop of emerging possibility. To control, to expand and expel that energy, which could be abused, for the sake of pure creation and beauty. Amendment to her dreams. The dreams of a dreamer in a permanent fugue state.

She has white, long, hair. She lies in fetal-esque position, her very mind plugged into a supercomputer of some kind. Sort of cables, running from her head.

And the universe is her dream. The cells in her mind are the stars in the sky. She is the dreamer, the one who lies asleep, yet ever so awake.
